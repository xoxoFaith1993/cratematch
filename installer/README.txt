CrateMatch
==========

Turns a client's song list into a VirtualDJ playlist, using the music you already have.

HOW TO USE
1. Get the client's songs, one of these ways:
   - Spotify tab: click "Sign in with Spotify" once. Your playlists show up; click one.
   - Or paste a Spotify playlist link and click "Find songs".
   - Text list or document tab: paste the list, or click "Open a file..." for a .txt,
     .csv or Word .docx file. One song per line, like "Artist - Title". Songs listed
     under a "Do not play" heading are left out.
2. CrateMatch looks through your music (VirtualDJ's library, plus any folders you add
   with "Music folders...").
3. Check the list:
   - green  = found
   - yellow = probably right, worth a quick look
   - red    = you don't have it ("Copy missing songs" copies them so you can get them)
   Right-click a song to pick a different version (clean, extended, remix...) or to
   leave it out. Tick "Prefer clean versions" for family events.
4. Click "Create VirtualDJ playlist". In VirtualDJ it's under My Lists on the left.
   If VirtualDJ was already open, click the little "-" next to My Lists, then "+".

PARTS OF THE NIGHT (COCKTAIL HOUR, RECEPTION, FIRST DANCE...)
- If the client's list has headings like "Cocktail hour:" or "Reception", the songs are
  grouped by part, and VirtualDJ gets one list for the event with a sub-list for each
  part (click the + next to it). Untick "Sub-list per section" for one plain list.
- Client sent one Spotify playlist per part? Paste all the links at once, or load one
  and then load the next: CrateMatch asks whether to add it as another sub-list.
- Want to split a single playlist into parts yourself? Click "Edit as text", type
  headings like "First Dance:" above the songs, then click "Find these songs".

SIGNING IN WITH SPOTIFY
1. Click "Sign in with Spotify". A small Spotify window pops up.
2. Log in the way you always do (email, or Continue with Google / Facebook / Apple).
3. Click "Agree". The window closes by itself and your playlists show up.
You stay signed in. Spotify asks you to sign in again roughly every 6 months.

If CrateMatch says your Spotify "isn't on the list yet":
1. Find the email on your Spotify account: in the Spotify app click your picture
   (top right) > Account, then "Edit profile". If you log in with Google, Facebook
   or Apple, it's that account's email.
2. Send that email to the person who set up CrateMatch for you (the "Copy a
   message" button makes a text for you).
3. When they say you're added, click "Sign in with Spotify" again.
Until then, pasting a Spotify playlist link still works.

SOMETHING WRONG? CLICK "REPORT A PROBLEM"
The big button at the top right sends a message straight to the person who made
CrateMatch. Say what happened and what you were trying to do. It also sends the song
list you were working on (song names only) so the problem can be found and fixed.
If an error pops up, it offers to send a report for you.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Your music files are never moved, renamed or changed.
- Spotify playlists that someone else made: Spotify only lets apps read the first 100
  songs. For a longer one, open it in the Spotify app, click a song, press Ctrl+A then
  Ctrl+C, and paste into the "Text list or document" box.
- CrateMatch updates itself: when a new version is out, it downloads it as it opens
  and reopens by itself.
- Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher the first time: click
  "More info" > "Run anyway". It isn't signed, that's all.
